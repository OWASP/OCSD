# Objectives for Module 11 – Business Logic Security

This module covers flaws in an application's business rules and workflows that let users obtain outcomes the business did not intend.

A successful candidate can:

- Recognize business logic flaws and why they depend on the application's intended behavior rather than on a specific technology
- Identify business rules that must be enforced on the server, including values that must be calculated rather than accepted from the client
- Recognize workflow and state flaws, such as skipped steps, invalid state transitions, and repeated or out-of-order requests
- Recognize race conditions in business operations and distinguish controls that make check-and-act sequences atomic
- Identify abuse of business features such as discounts, referrals, reservations, and refunds, and the limits that prevent it
- Recognize automated threats against business flows and the strengths and limits of anti-automation controls
- Identify how abuse cases, business event logging, and monitoring help detect business logic abuse
- Identify test cases that reveal business logic flaws

## Expected competency

Candidates can recognize whether an application enforces its business rules and workflows under unexpected use.
They can identify client-controlled values, skippable steps, race conditions, and missing limits that allow abuse.
