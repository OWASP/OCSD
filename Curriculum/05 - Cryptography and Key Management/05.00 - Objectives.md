# Objectives for Module 5 – Cryptography and Key Management

This module covers the cryptographic protections web applications rely on and the management of the keys that support them.

A successful candidate can:

- Distinguish encryption, cryptographic hashing, and encoding
- Identify the protections provided by authenticated encryption, message authentication codes, and digital signatures
- Recognize common cryptographic choices and the risks of obsolete algorithms, unsafe modes, and custom implementations
- Identify what makes cryptographic keys resistant to brute-force guessing
- Recognize when cryptographic values need unpredictability, uniqueness, or both
- Distinguish the roles of key agreement and certificates in establishing trusted communication
- Identify safeguards for cryptographic keys from generation through retirement and destruction
- Recognize the risks of failed verification, key exposure, and loss of access to keys

## Expected competency

Candidates can recognize whether a web application design uses cryptography appropriate for its security needs.
They can identify weaknesses in how the application generates random values, verifies protected data, establishes trust, and manages its keys.
